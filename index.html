DBI = percentile of DBI₀ against all 420 possible answer patterns

Averaging twenty answers collapses the spread, and the raw composite has a standard deviation of only 10.4, which would put most possible responses between 40 and 60 and make the gauge useless
The headline is therefore a percentile, so 80 means your reading is later in the cycle than 80 percent of all possible answer patterns
